# Nakamura-Steinsson 2018 Concept

- To our knowledge, endogenous rates + VAR residual confounding is the incumbent defect; the HF FOMC window is the fix.
- The power trade-off is first-class: ~5 bp shocks means inference must live in contemporaneous financial objects, not quarterly output paths.
- As far as we know, the punchline couples short-horizon real-rate non-neutrality (at least 106 bp on 2y real, scaled) with an information channel.
