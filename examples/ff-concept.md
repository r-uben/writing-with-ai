# Fama-French 1997 Concept

- Textbooks stress cash-flow uncertainty; to the best of our knowledge, they understate discount-rate estimation error.
- The CAPM remains standard but contested; the FF3 fits better in sample but is empirically motivated. As far as we are aware, both give fat industry bands.
- There are two stacked errors: factor-premium sampling error and industry loading imprecision.
- Punchline: industry standard errors are at least 3.0% per year. This at least suggests that firm/project estimates are worse, and NPV precision is partly illusory unless estimation risk enters the decision.
