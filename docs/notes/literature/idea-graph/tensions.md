## Closing — kinds of tension this batch surfaces

1. **Method-vs-construct fights** dominate the densest cluster (KS↔ST): connectivity, quoting, and ownership interviews are attacked as bridges to “cognitive debt/agency,” more often than raw group differences are denied.  
2. **Only two hard `contradicts` edges** land (debt gradient; impaired-learning summary) — the rest of ST→KS is `critiques`.  
3. **Homogenization is a cross-domain extension family** (PH/DH/KS) with an **individual-benefit scope wall** (DH vs PH quality story), not a single contradictory fact.  
4. **Persuasion lineage is extension-heavy** (JK→WC); tension appears at **mitigation efficacy** (failed Warning/Debrief vs “monitor opinions”), not at whether attitude shift occurs.  
5. **Inheritance edges matter:** KB→PH `depends_on` and PH→JK `depends_on` block accidental double-counting of cited priors as new support.  
6. **Shared gaps** (longitudinal persistence) align quietly across homogenization and persuasion papers — a different tension type than pairwise contradiction.
