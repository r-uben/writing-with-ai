## What our framing may miss (hypothesis only)

Batch-1 orphans cluster less around “missing writing-quality themes” and more around **dynamics, credit, and costs outside the prose object**: (1) **temporal feedback** — single-session diversity optimism vs unstudied repeated user–model loops; (2) **ownership/credit as a distinct axis** from cognitive agency (evaluator penalty ≠ writer self-ownership); (3) **strategic collective dilemmas** that sit next to homogenization but are not the same claim; (4) **energy/material cost** of LLM writing as a non-cognitive externality; (5) method orphans (EEG pipeline, unsegmented writing stages, comedy null) that mark where educational-cognition papers outrun the README’s writing-skill framing. If diagnosis later treats these as noise, the graph will under-detect questions about longitudinal imprint, credit norms, and non-textual costs of AI writing assistance.
